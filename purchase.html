PIXELLE Snap is a pocket-sized Bluetooth printer that turns photos on your phone into full-colour, peel-and-stick prints. Features include:

  • Zero-ink printing: colour is built into the paper

  • 2×3" sticky-backed prints that are smudge-proof, water-resistant and tear-resistant

  • About 45 seconds per print, 25 prints per charge

  • Free PIXELLE app with filters, frames, captions and collage split

  • Party Mode for up to four phones

Snap is a great choice for journalers, party hosts, families and small sellers who want their photos off the screen and into their hands.
