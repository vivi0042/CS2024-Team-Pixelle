FEATURE HIGHLIGHT

ZERO-INK TECHNOLOGY

Colour crystals live inside every sheet. Snap's print head heats them with precise pulses to bring out millions of colours. There are no cartridges to buy and nothing to dry out in Singapore's humidity.

SMUDGE-PROOF & WATER-RESISTANT

Prints come out dry to the touch and survive gel pens, sticky fingers and a rainy walk to class. They're tear-resistant too, so they last on laptops and water bottles.

PRINTS IN ABOUT 45 SECONDS

Tap print and Snap is done before the group photo is over. Ten sheets fit in the tray, and a refill takes five seconds.

PARTY MODE FOR UP TO 4 PHONES

Everyone at the table can send photos to one printer over Bluetooth 5.0. Use the free PIXELLE app on iPhone and Android, with no account needed to start.

POCKET-SIZED, 175 G

Lighter than a can of kopi at 12 × 7.8 × 2.4 cm. A 90-minute USB-C charge gives about 25 prints, enough for a whole birthday party.
