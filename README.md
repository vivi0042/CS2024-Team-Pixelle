**PIXELLE – Portable Photo Sticker Printer Website**

For our CS2024 Web Design and Technologies final group project, we developed **PIXELLE**, a fictional lifestyle brand offering a portable photo sticker printer designed for journaling and scrapbooking enthusiasts.

Our website combines a nostalgic, retro-inspired aesthetic with modern web design to showcase the product, highlight its features, and encourage customers to transform their digital memories into tangible keepsakes. Developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the website focuses on intuitive navigation, responsive design, user experience, and effective marketing strategies to promote product sales.
